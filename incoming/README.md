# Drop original photos here

Upload full-size photos into the matching folder. They are not shown on the site directly:
Claude resizes them, removes location data and places them in `public/photos/`.

- `hero/`: one favourite couple photo for the top of the page
- `wedding/`: 6–12 wedding photos (the first is used as the big chapter background)
- `2022/` … `2026/`: one strong photo per year
- `ahead/`: two photos or mood pictures for the "road ahead" section
- `letter/`: one calm photo behind the closing letter
- `music/`: optional MP3 of your song

Captions and dates can go in a message to Claude, or in `notes.txt` inside the folder.
